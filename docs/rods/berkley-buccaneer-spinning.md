# Berkley Buccaneer B95 - spinning rod

P.: (initial AI draft intro text for page)

"The Berkley Buccaneer B95 is a 7 ft 6 in, two-piece fiberglass spinning rod rated for 1 – 2 oz lures. The yellow-and-dark-brown color scheme is a good representative of Berkley’s robust saltwater-oriented fiberglass rods of the period: substantial enough for heavier plugs and shore-oriented work, yet short enough for general-purpose use.

This example retains unusually clean original cosmetics, including its decals, wraps, cork grip, guides, and metal reel seat. It is being documented as a period companion to a Penn 710 spinning reel."
