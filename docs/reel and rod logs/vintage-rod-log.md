# Vintage rod log

## St. Croix Pacemaker 400 HD

**Purchased**: 8.19.26, $9.98, ebay, seller: `tf*finds`.

**Description**: 4' 6" one-piece fiberglass baitcasting rod, teal with dark blue guide wraps

**Condition**: appears blemish-free in seller images

### Evaluation

Overall: very good, perfectly functional with minor blemishes

Blank: very good, a few minor scratches

Guides: light rust

Localized rust and plating loss on frame, particularly near the lower shoulder / foot area. Most of the rust is on the inside of the frames.

Tip is almost rust-free, shows remnants of a clear amber coating, mostly gone. Bit of this appear on the other guides too.

Reel seat: light wear with some bare metal showing through paint

Line wraps: very good

Original blue wraps with light trim remain in excellent condition.

Decals: fair with some worn spots

Locking nut for blank: unscrews OK. Blank to reel seat connection looks good, minimal corrosion 4 metal strips.

Reel lock: works, utilizes a rubber knob and screw head. Some minor rust.

Butt cap: Original, very dark blue hard rubber.

### Restoration / conservation

The whole rod was given a scrub with a soft brush and very diluted Dawn detergent.

The hard rubber was cleaned with #0000 steel wool and fiber pads and given a light coating of '303' rubber preservative.

The guides were lightly cleaned with #0000 steel wool and a wooden pick. Some of the rust is very difficult to get to, so was left alone rather than risk damage to the guide wraps.
