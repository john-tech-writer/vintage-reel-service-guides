# Vintage reel parts

This page lists external sources that may be useful when locating replacement
parts for vintage fishing reels. Listings are provided for information only.
Availability, compatibility, prices, materials, and seller policies may change.
Inclusion does not imply endorsement.

A range of parts sources will be included on this page, so here are some definitions to clarify the terminology.

## Terminology

| Term                | Meaning in this page                              |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Original part       | The component originally supplied by reel maker         |
| OEM replacement     | A replacement made or officially supplied by reel maker |
| NOS                 | New-old-stock original reel maker inventory             |
| Donor part          | An original used part removed from another reel   |
| Non-OEM replacement | A compatible part made by an independent maker    |

This page will only list suppliers that the Vintage Reels project has obtained parts, installed them in reels, tested them, and found them on that limited basis to be resources worth including here. Parts and suppliers are listed by repairs actually performed in the guides.
