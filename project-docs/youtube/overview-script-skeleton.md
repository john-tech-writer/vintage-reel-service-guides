*opening shot, pause a moment. general intro, reel intro, duty rating, maker, production years*

Hello, and welcome to the Vintage Reel Service Guides. This video is a brief overview of the Pflueger Nobby, a sporty-to-heavy-duty baitcasting reel made by the Pflueger Fishing Tackle company starting in 1935.

*pick up reel. where it sat in the maker's lineup, fresh / salt, type of fishing, typical fish*

This is a very light reel, rustpoof and designed for both freshwater and saltwater use--from bass and pike all the way up to muskies and tarpon.
This is model number 1963--

*point to logo / badge. variants.*

There were several models with different colors and features over the years, but the basic mechanics of the reel stayed the same.

*point to spool. specs.*

The Nobby can hold up to 175 yards of monofilament, between 12 and 30 pound test, and up to 290 yards of braided line. It has an aluminum spool and this model features an arbor made of cork which makes it possible to cast lighter lures.

*operating features, demo.*

It also features an adjustable anti-backlash for casting lures of different weights--you just turn this button to adjust tension on the spool--you can feel it in the handle as you turn the button--

*demo button, handle*

The level-wind mechanism winds the line evenly onto the spool on retrieve, and there's even an extra part for the mechanism stored in a small compartment in the side of the cap.

*point to level wind, turn handle*

The clicker is also easy to engage using the button on the left side. You can use this as a strike indicator or to keep the spool from turning to keep the line tight.

*point, demo*

*wrap-up, finishes, materials.*

The design is very simple and sturdy. It has a satin finish, aluminum body, and brass gears that you can see in the service video. 

*pointer to service content*

In this channel, the Nobby is one of the pattern reels used for learning basic service procedures. If you’d like to see how to take one apart, service it, and put it back together, there’s a separate quick service video plus a more detailed, fully illustrated guide linked in the description.
