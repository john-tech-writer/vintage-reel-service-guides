# Cleaning and lubrication for vintage reels - YouTube video description

Title: Cleaning and Lubrication for Vintage Reels

URL: https://youtu.be/LsqoT7lD7cw 

In this reference video, we walk through a standard procedure for cleaning and lubricating mid-priced American-made fishing reels (spinning, spincast, baitcasting, and fly) from the 1930s to the 1970s.

This is the cleaning and lubrication method used in the reel-specific service series (Penn, Shakespeare, and others). If you’re watching a “Part 2 – Disassembling the Gears” video, follow this reference for cleaning and lubrication unless that video notes a reel?specific exception.

What this covers:

  – Tools, solvents, and lubricants for vintage reels
  – Degreasing and drying parts
  – Where to use oil vs. grease, and how much
  – Final wipe?down and storage tips

About this series

This video is part of an ongoing series on servicing mid-priced American-made fishing reels from the 1930s--1970s, including spinning, spincast, baitcasting, and fly models. Each reel has a matching written guide so you can follow along at the bench with clear photos and on-step-at-a-time instructions.

Disclaimers

Servicing vintage reels involves small parts and occasionally fragile components. Proceed at your own risk and work carefully--replacement parts for most models may be limited or unavailable.
