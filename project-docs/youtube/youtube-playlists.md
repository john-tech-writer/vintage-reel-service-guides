`[maker] Reels - Overviews`

Penn Reels - Overviews
Pflueger Reels - Overviews

*Usage: all overviews for a specific maker. On YT front page.*

Overviews of mid-priced, mid-century American reels made by Pflueger, including history, features, and duty ratings. 

These short videos look at where the reels fit in Pflueger's family of reels, what they are commonly used for, and why these reels are worthy of not only preserving, but using in the field. 

The companion Vintage Reel Service Guides site provides more detailed overviews of each reel as well as step-by-step illustrated service guides you can follow at the bench. All types of reels are covered, including spinning, spincast (push-button), baitcasting, and fly reels.

More narrative content about the reels and the creation of the videos is offered on the Vintage Reel Service Substack.

Penn Reels - Service Guides
Pflueger Reels - Service Guides

*Usage: all service guides for a specific maker.*

Service videos for vintage Pflueger reels, organized by model, from initial teardown through final testing. There are also dedicated playlists for each reel, for example "Pflueger Nobby model No. 1963 - Full Video Series," which include an overview video about the reel's history and features.

Each service series is organized into several (usually 2) videos, plus a general purpose cleaning and lubrication video, for example:

1. Pflueger Nobby model No. 1963 - Service Part 1 - Disassembly (Baitcasting Reel, 1930s–70s)

2. Pflueger Nobby model No. 1963 - Service Part 2 - Reassembly (Baitcasting Reel, 1930s–70s) 

3. Cleaning and Lubrication for Vintage Reels
