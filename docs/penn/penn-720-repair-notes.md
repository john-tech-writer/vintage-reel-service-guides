# Penn 720 - Repair notes

## Crosswind block replacement

**Project-reel history**: During field testing in August 2026, the original crosswind block in the project Penn 720 cracked. The immediate symptom was that the crank would not turn in either direction, indicating an internal bind.

An epoxy repair of the original crosswind block was attempted but failed.

**Original part**: The crosswind block's original Penn part no. is 43-720. It is one of the very few plastic components used in Penn 720, 720Z, 722, and 722Z reels. This part may be more susceptible than the metal parts to cracking and failure with normal wear, age, and field use. The original block is made of white plastic.

**Replacement part**: Replacement parts for vintage reels are often difficult to locate and are often transplanted from donor "parts" reels. In this case, however, a new, non-OEM replacement crosswind block was located and obtained for the repair. See [Reel parts](reel-parts.md) for details. The replacement block obtained for this repair is made of black carbon fiber.

**Installation observations**: The replacement crosswind block appeared virtually identical to the original part except for the color. There were some very small bits of stray material here and there which were easily removed with light tooling.

**Testing:** The replacement fit the reel and restored normal operation during initial bench testing. While the initial outcome was good, the reel has not yet been field-tested and long-term durability has not yet been evaluated.

**Field-test record**: Not yet field tested.
